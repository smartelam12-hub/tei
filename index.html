About TEI PACKAGE

TEI PACKAGE is a premier industrial packaging manufacturer and supply chain partner based in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, India. Situated at the heart of South India's major automotive and manufacturing corridor, we supply returnable and heavy-duty packaging systems to OEMs, Tier-1 component makers, and logistics warehouses.

Our capabilities include injection molded plastic crates, high-impact storage bins, foldable large containers (FLC), ESD anti-static packaging for electronics, PP corrugated boxes, and custom EPE foam fitments tailored to client component geometries.
